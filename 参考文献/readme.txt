This document contains the contents of the comments located in the Final_AutoData.csv, Agg_dataS.csv, and Agg_dataC.csv files.



ID = Sample number

Meas = Effect size number within a sample

Published = Publication status. 1 = Published, 0 = Unpublished

DiagStatus = Diagnostic Status: proportion of those in the depression group with formal MDD diagnoses

DepStatus = Depression Status: proportion of those in the depression group who were currently depressed vs. remitted	

nDep = Sample size of depression group 	

nCon = Sample size of control group	

ageDep = Mean age of depression group	

ageCon = Mean age of control group	

sex = Proportion of females in the depression group	

edu = Mean years of education of depression group	

matchAge = Whether depression and control groups were age matched. 1 = matched, 0 = not matched	

matchEdu = Whether depression and control groups were education matched. 1 = matched, 0 = not matched	

IQDep = IQ of depression group	

episode = Proportion of those in the depression group with recurrent (vs. first episode) depression	

onsetAge = Age of Onset: age of depression onset given in the study	

onsetAgeCalc = Age of Onset (calculated from Duration): age of depression onset provided in the paper + values calculated from time since diagnosis		

duration = Time Since Diagnosis: mean time (in months) since individual was diagnosed with depression	

durationCalc = Time Since Diagnosis (calculated from Age of Onset: mean time (in months) since depression diagnosis provided in the paper + values calculated from age of onset 	

med = Medication: proportion of individuals taking medication for depression		

Recruitment = Recruitment Site. 1 = Patient, 0 = Community / University

MADRS = Mean score of the depressed group on MADRS questionnaire	

HDRS = Mean score of the depressed group on HDRS questionnaire	

BDI = Mean score of the depressed group on BDI questionnaire	

BDICalc = Transformed mean symptom severity scores	

HADS = Mean score of the depressed group on HADS questionnaire

CESD = Mean score of the depressed group on CESD questionnaire

GDS-30 = Mean score of the depressed group on GDS-30 questionnaire	

SDS = Mean score of the depressed group on SDS questionnaire

DID = Mean score of the depressed group on DID questionnaire

MASQ = Mean score of the depressed group on MASQ questionnaire

d = Cohen’s d = (depressed group mean - control group mean)/pooled SD. Larger magntiude negative values for specificity indicate worse specificity for depressed groups, while larger magnitude positive values for categoricity indicate higher categoricity for depressed groups.

cp = 1-(3/4 df-1), correction for small sample size. 	

g = Hedges’ g = d[1 – (3/4 df – 1)], where df is the degrees of freedom of the entire sample.	

varg = Variability of g	

Stat used = Statistic used to calculate effect sizes

AMT = Whether the effect size came from the AMT. 1 = AMT, 0 = Non-AMT

Positive = Valence of stimuli. 1 = positive, 0 = neutral or negative 	

Neutral	= Valence of stimuli. 1 = neutral, 0 = positive or negative

Negative = Valence of stimuli. 1 = negative, 0 = positive or neutral	

Specific = Whether the effect size is for specificity. 1 = specificity. 0 = categoricity

Categoric = Whether the effect size is for categoricity. 1 = categoricity. 0 = specificity
