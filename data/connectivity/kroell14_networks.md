# Kroell et al. (2024): 14 Canonical Functional Networks

## Source

The author name is **Jean-Philippe Kroell** (also spelled Kröll), from the Institute of
Neuroscience and Medicine (INM-1), Research Centre Juelich, Germany. The manuscript is
deposited at Juelich: https://juser.fz-juelich.de/record/1006698

The paper defines 14 meta-analytically derived functional networks, each with a specific
set of brain regions (nodes). These networks were constructed by synthesizing prior
coordinate-based meta-analyses from the neuroimaging literature.

## Related Publication

The closely related preprint defining the 13 co-activation networks from the same group is:

- **Dugré JR, Potvin S** (2023). Towards a Neurobiologically-driven Ontology of Mental
  Functions: A Data-driven Summary of the Twenty Years of Neuroimaging Meta-Analyses.
  bioRxiv 2023.03.29.534795. doi: 10.1101/2023.03.29.534795

The Kroell manuscript builds on this by providing 14 functionally-defined networks
optimized for connectivity analysis.

---

## Network 1: Autobiographical Memory (AM) — 22 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Medial temporal cortex / hippocampus / parahippocampus
- Lateral temporal cortex
- Precuneus / posterior cingulate cortex / retrosplenial cortex
- Temporo-parietal junction (TPJ)
- Lateral prefrontal cortex (dlPFC)
- Lateral occipital cortex
- Medial prefrontal cortex (mPFC)

**Functional role:** Recall of personally experienced past events (episodic memory),
self-projection, scene construction.

---

## Network 2: Cognitive Attention Control (CogAC) — 19 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Anterior insula (bilateral)
- Inferior frontal gyrus (IFG)
- Dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (dlPFC)
- Dorsal premotor cortex
- Intraparietal sulcus (IPS, bilateral)
- Superior parietal lobule
- Right temporo-parietal junction (rTPJ)
- Left inferior occipital gyrus
- Pre-supplementary motor area (pre-SMA)
- Anterior mid-cingulate cortex (aMCC)
- Right thalamus
- Right caudate

**Functional role:** Goal-directed attention, cognitive control, task switching.

---

## Network 3: Extended Multiple Demand Network (eMDN) — 17 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Bilateral inferior frontal gyrus (IFG)
- Insula (bilateral)
- Supplementary motor area (SMA)
- Intraparietal sulcus (bilateral)
- Middle frontal gyrus
- Dorsal premotor cortex
- Putamen (bilateral)
- Thalamus (bilateral)
- Left inferior temporal gyrus

**Reference:** Camilleri et al. (2016)

**Functional role:** Executive functions, cognitive flexibility, task-demand adaptation.

---

## Network 4: Emotional Scene and Face Processing (EmoSF) — 24 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Medial prefrontal cortex
- Bilateral inferior/middle/superior frontal gyrus
- Amygdala (bilateral)
- Parahippocampal gyrus
- Fusiform gyrus (includes FFA)
- Orbitofrontal cortex
- Lateral occipital cortex
- Thalamus / pulvinar
- Right middle temporal gyrus
- Right anterior cingulate cortex

**Functional role:** Perception of emotional faces and scenes, affective salience detection.

---

## Network 5: Empathy — 18 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Bilateral dorsomedial prefrontal cortex (dmPFC)
- Anterior insula (bilateral)
- Inferior frontal gyrus (bilateral)
- Supplementary motor area (SMA)
- Cingulate cortex (aMCC / ACC)
- Temporo-parietal junction (TPJ)
- Right amygdala
- Right middle temporal gyrus
- Right posterior superior temporal sulcus (pSTS)
- Left anterior thalamus
- Right posterior thalamus
- Right hippocampus
- Midbrain
- Right pallidum

**Functional role:** Empathic understanding, affective sharing, mentalizing about others' pain.

---

## Network 6: Emotion Regulation (ER) — 14 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Bilateral inferior/middle frontal gyrus (vlPFC, dlPFC)
- Superior parietal lobule
- Right amygdala
- Right medial frontal gyrus
- Left anterior cingulate cortex
- Left anterior insula
- Left superior/middle temporal gyrus

**Functional role:** Cognitive reappraisal, emotion suppression, regulation of affective responses.

---

## Network 7: Extended Socio-Affective Default Network (eSAD) — 12 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Anterior cingulate cortex (ACC)
- Bilateral amygdala and hippocampus
- Temporo-parietal junction (TPJ)
- Ventral basal ganglia (ventral striatum)
- Precuneus
- Subgenual cingulate cortex
- Ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC)
- Dorsomedial prefrontal cortex (dmPFC)
- Left middle temporal gyrus and sulcus

**Reference:** Amft et al. (2015)

**Functional role:** Self-referential thought, social cognition, affective processing,
introspection, autobiographical memory.

---

## Network 8: Mirror Neuron System (MNS) — 11 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Bilateral inferior frontal gyrus (pars opercularis, BA44)
- Primary somatosensory cortex (S1)
- Lateral occipital lobe
- Right fusiform face/body area
- Left medial premotor cortex
- Left posterior middle temporal gyrus (pMTG)
- Right superior parietal lobule

**Functional role:** Action observation, imitation, understanding others' actions.

---

## Network 9: Motor — 9 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Bilateral sensorimotor cortex (M1/S1)
- Basal ganglia (putamen, pallidum)
- Anterior cerebellum (Lobule IV-V)
- Inferior parietal cortex
- Left ventral premotor cortex
- Supplementary motor area (SMA)

**Functional role:** Motor execution, movement planning.

---

## Network 10: Reward (Rew) — 23 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Bilateral insula
- Thalamus (bilateral)
- Brainstem (VTA / substantia nigra)
- Mid-orbitofrontal cortex (mOFC)
- Middle frontal gyrus
- Right nucleus accumbens
- Left pallidum
- Left dorsomedial prefrontal cortex
- Left medial orbitofrontal cortex
- Right amygdala
- Supplementary motor area (SMA)
- Anterior/posterior cingulate cortex
- Left inferior parietal lobule (IPL)
- Right angular gyrus
- Left frontal pole
- Left superior frontal gyrus

**Functional role:** Reward processing, motivation, hedonic evaluation.

---

## Network 11: Semantic Memory (SM) — 23 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Angular gyrus / supramarginal gyrus
- Middle temporal gyrus
- Posterior inferior temporal gyrus
- Mid-fusiform gyrus
- Parahippocampus
- Dorsomedial prefrontal cortex (dmPFC)
- Ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC)
- Orbital prefrontal cortex
- Superior/middle/inferior frontal gyrus
- Posterior cingulate gyrus
- Ventral precuneus

**Reference:** Binder et al. (2009)

**Functional role:** Conceptual knowledge, word meaning, factual memory.

---

## Network 12: Theory of Mind (ToM) — 15 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC)
- Dorsomedial prefrontal cortex (dmPFC)
- Frontopolar cortex (BA10)
- Precuneus
- Bilateral temporo-parietal junction (TPJ)
- Temporal pole
- Middle temporal gyrus
- Posterior superior temporal sulcus (pSTS)
- Inferior frontal gyrus
- Right V5/MT+

**Functional role:** Mentalizing, belief attribution, understanding others' intentions
and mental states.

---

## Network 13: Vigilant Attention (VigAtt) — 16 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Anterior paracentral lobule
- Right medial posterior superior frontal gyrus
- Dorsal mid-cingulate cortex (dMCC)
- Bilateral inferior frontal junction
- Anterior insula (bilateral)
- Thalamus (bilateral)
- Right inferior frontal sulcus
- Left precentral gyrus
- Left inferior occipital gyrus
- Right temporo-parietal junction (rTPJ)
- Right middle occipital gyrus
- Right inferior parietal lobule (IPL)
- Cerebellum

**Functional role:** Sustained attention, alertness, vigilance decrement resistance.

---

## Network 14: Working Memory (WM) — 23 nodes

**Brain regions:**
- Bilateral anterior insula
- Inferior frontal gyrus (bilateral)
- Caudal lateral PFC (dlPFC)
- Rostral lateral PFC
- Posterior superior frontal gyrus (pre-SMA / SMA)
- Thalamus (bilateral)
- Cerebellum (Lobule VI / Crus I)
- Intraparietal sulcus (IPS, bilateral)
- Superior parietal lobule
- Left caudate
- Left globus pallidus

**Functional role:** Maintenance and manipulation of information online, cognitive
control, executive function.
