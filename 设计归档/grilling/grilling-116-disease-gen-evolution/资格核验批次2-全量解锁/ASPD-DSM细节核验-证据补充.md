## 一、DSM-5 Section II ASPD 门槛结构（A/B/C/D + 逐条）

**命题 1-A（门槛）**：ASPD = Criterion A（自 15 岁以来的普遍漠视/侵犯他人权利模式，7 条中 ≥3 条，polythetic）+ B（≥18 岁）+ C（15 岁前品行障碍证据）+ D（反社会行为非仅在精神分裂症或双相障碍病程中出现）。

**核验结果**：确认（两版一致；镜像间措辞小异）。

- 英文（DSM-5-TR，StatPearls 转述）："A pervasive pattern of disregard for and violation of the rights of others, occurring since age 15 years, as indicated by 3 (or more) of the following"
- 英文（DSM-5 2013，SAMHSA Exhibit 4.7 转述，引 "APA (2013, p. 659). Reprinted with permission from the DSM-5 (Copyright © 2013)"）："A. A pervasive pattern of disregard for and violation of the rights of others, occurring since age 15 years, as indicated by three (or more) of the following:"

**命题 1-B（A 的 7 条逐条）**：
1. "Failure to conform to social norms concerning lawful behaviors, as indicated by repeatedly performing acts that are grounds for arrest."（StatPearls 转述 TR；Exhibit 4.7 作 "...with respect to lawful behaviors..."）
2. "Deceitfulness, as indicated by repeated lying, use of aliases, or conning others for personal profit or pleasure."
3. "Impulsivity or failure to plan ahead."
4. "Irritability and aggressiveness, as indicated by repeated physical fights or assaults."
5. "Reckless disregard for the safety of self or others."
6. "Consistent irresponsibility, as indicated by repeated failure to sustain consistent work behavior or honor financial obligations."
7. "Lack of remorse, as indicated by being indifferent to or rationalizing having hurt, mistreated, or stolen from another."

来源：
- [StatPearls — Antisocial Personality Disorder（DSM-5-TR Criteria 块）](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK546673/)
- [NCBI Bookshelf NBK571021（SAMHSA TIP 42, Updated 2020）Exhibit 4.7（DSM-5 2013 逐字镜像）](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK571021/box/ch4.b23/?report=objectonly)

**命题 1-C（B/C/D 逐字）**：
- B（DSM-5 2013 Exhibit 4.7）：“B. The individual is at least age 18 years.”（StatPearls TR 转述作 "The individual is at least 18 years old." —— 措辞小异，非逐字）
- C（两版同义）：“C. There is evidence of conduct disorder with onset before age 15 years.”（StatPearls TR 转述作 "Evidence is present of conduct disorder with onset before age 15 years."）
- D（两版同义）：“D. The occurrence of antisocial behavior is not exclusively during the course of schizophrenia or bipolar disorder.”

**命题 1-D（重点核验：D 措辞版次差异）**：DSM-5(2013) 是否写 "schizophrenia or a manic episode"，DSM-5-TR(2022) 是否改 "schizophrenia or bipolar disorder"？

核验结果：**未获支持。证据指向 DSM-5(2013) 与 DSM-5-TR 均写 "schizophrenia or bipolar disorder"；"a manic episode" 为 DSM-IV(-TR) 措辞**。依据（均为 2022 前、引 DSM-5-2013 的镜像）：
1. SAMHSA TIP 42 "Updated 2020"（早于 DSM-5-TR），其 Chapter 4 明言使用 "the latest criteria ... published in ... DSM-5 (APA, 2013)"，Exhibit 4.7 引 "APA (2013, p. 659)"，D = "...schizophrenia or bipolar disorder."
2. Vanderbilt 精神病学 morning report 教学 PDF（2014–15 学年）逐字列 DSM-5 准则 A–D，D = "...schizophrenia or bipolar disorder."
3. MUSC ECHO-ASPD-OUD 幻灯片（"DSM 5 criteria"），D = "Symptoms do not occur exclusively during the course of schizophrenia or bipolar disorder."
4. Carepatron "DSM 5 Diagnostic Criteria" 讲义，D = "...not exclusively during the course of schizophrenia or bipolar disorder."
5. 反证锚：Lumen 《Abnormal Psychology》页面标题即为 "DSM-IV-TR criteria"，其 D = "...during the course of Schizophrenia or a Manic Episode."（即 "manic episode" 版本出自 DSM-IV-TR 系文本）；Behavenet 301.7 页 2016 快照（[DSM-IV 版 URL](https://webarchive.loc.gov/all/20160912194650/https://behavenet.com/node/21650)）亦命中 "a manic episode" 短语。
6. 德语维基 Dissoziale Persönlichkeitsstörung §4.1 DSM-5 德译准则 D = "...nicht ausschließlich im Verlauf einer Schizophrenie oder einer bipolaren Störung"（bipolarer Störung）——与上述一致。

结论（材料层）：用户清单第 1 条预设的 "DSM-5(2013)=manic episode → DSM-5-TR=bipolar disorder" **大概率不成立**；真实分界在 DSM-IV(-TR)（manic episode）与 DSM-5/DSM-5-TR（bipolar disorder）之间。最终裁决 [需核验]：APA 印刷版 DSM-5(2013) p.659 与 DSM-5-TR p.771 对页逐字。

## 二、adult antisocial behavior（V/Z 码，非正式诊断）

**命题 2**：DSM-5 文本体系为"无 15 岁前品行障碍证据、但其余符合 ASPD 表象的成人反社会行为"单设编码（V/Z 码），非正式精神障碍诊断。

核验结果：**确认（StatPearls 逐字 + ICD-10-CM 编码表佐证）**。

- StatPearls（DSM-5-TR 语境，逐字）：“Adults who do not have evidence of conduct disorder in childhood but otherwise meet the criteria for ASPD can be diagnosed with adult antisocial behavior. While this is not a formal DSM-5-TR diagnosis, the diagnosis is listed as a 'V code' (in ICD-9) or 'Z code' (in ICD-10), which are used to identify factors influencing a patient's health status.”
- ICD-10-CM 码号：**Z72.811 "Antisocial behavior, adult"**（组目 Z72.81 Antisocial behavior；同一组的儿童/青少年条目为 Z72.810）。来源：[ICD10data Z72.811](https://www.icd10data.com/ICD10CM/Codes/Z00-Z99/Z69-Z76/Z72-/Z72.811)、[AAPC Codify Z72.811](https://www.aapc.com/codes/icd-10-codes/Z72.811)。
- DSM-IV-TR 版同义表述（Lumen 教材，二手）："When antisocial behavior occurs without any signs of it during adolescence, the DSM-IV diagnosis is Adult Antisocial Behavior."（对应 DSM-IV 的 V71.01 用法）

[需核验]：DSM-5(2013) 原书 "Other Conditions That May Be a Focus of Clinical Attention" 一节该条目**逐字**（免费源未取到；StatPearls/编码表为二手+官方码表佐证）。DSM-5 印刷期（ICD-9-CM）展示码 V71.01，对应 ICD-10-CM Z72.811 的 crosswalk 形式亦 [需核验]。

## 三、文化相关议题与鉴别诊断（含 SUD/BPD/CD/双相/精分）

**命题 3-A（文化/亚文化/环境适应性）**：反社会行为在不同文化、亚文化、环境（帮派、战区、贫困环境）中可能具适应性，判定时需警惕把"文化常态行为"当人格病理。

核验结果：DSM Section II ASPD 的 "Culture-Related Diagnostic Issues" 段落**未在任何免费权威镜像中取到逐字**（StatPearls 无 culture 节；MSD/Theravive 亦无）。[需核验]。可作材料的间接逐字锚：
- DSM-5 Section III AMPD ASPD 之 Criterion A 的 Self-direction 域定义含 "absence of prosocial internal standards, associated with failure to conform to lawful or **culturally normative** ethical behavior"（VUMC 镜像逐字；"culturally normative"一词官方文本可见）
- 二手立场文献（非 DSM 原文，仅作方向材料）：ASPD as adaptive strategy（[Semantic Scholar 条目](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Antisocial-Personality-Disorder-as-a-Form-of-Pyatnitskiy-Enikolopov/90e1eaf259af1ddf39819d78db09079b0a59abf2)）

**命题 3-B（鉴别诊断——反社会行为非 ASPD 专属）**：
- 双相/精神分裂症（“仅病程中”反社会 → 排除）：Criterion D 逐字（§一）即条款；StatPearls 补充："Irritability and aggression can occur in psychotic and bipolar spectrum illnesses. However, isolated ASPD is not associated with a decreased need for sleep, and the behavioral patterns are chronic rather than episodic, eg, manic or hypomanic episodes."（二手）
- SUD：StatPearls（二手）："chronic alcohol and drug use can contribute to antisocial behaviors. Differentiating this from ASPD, where the antisocial pattern persists through states of both intoxication and sobriety, is important."；MSD（二手）："Most patients also have a substance use disorder (and about half of those with a substance use disorder meet criteria for antisocial personality disorder)"。DSM-5 该段逐字（"antisocial behavior ... in the context of substance use ..."类措辞）[需核验]
- BPD：StatPearls（二手）："ASPD can be differentiated from BPD, as BPD is associated with much greater internal conflict, eg, fear of abandonment, identity issues, and frequent mood swings."；VUMC：Cluster B 间可共病同诊
- CD：DSM-5 CD Criterion C（CBHSQ 逐字，见 §五）：“If the individual is age 18 years or older, criteria are not met for Antisocial Personality Disorder.”——成年后 CD 与 ASPD 互斥、CD 是儿童期专属
- 犯罪/环境行为：VUMC 幻灯片（二手）：“Criminal behavior not associated with a personality disorder – ASPD must be distinguished from criminal behavior undertaken for gain that is not associated with [personality pathology]”（该页要点式，截断处 [需核验]）；StatPearls：“A thorough history is generally sufficient to distinguish ASPD from isolated acts of misbehavior, which are inconsistent with long-term antisocial traits.”
- ADHD：免费权威源未见 DSM-5 ASPD 鉴别段把 ADHD 列为正式条目；MSD 将 ADHD 列为共病（"often also have ... attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder"）。DSM 原文如何处理 ADHD [需核验]
- StatPearls 鉴别清单（二手）："BPD; NPD; Substance use disorders; Substance-induced antisocial behavior; Psychotic or mood disorders (eg, bipolar disorder); Intermittent explosive disorder; Medical conditions (eg, temporal lobe epilepsy ...); Isolated acts of misbehavior"

## 四、Section III 替代模型（AMPD）ASPD

**命题 4**：Section III AMPD ASPD = Criterion A（人格功能损害，Identity/Self-direction/Empathy/Intimacy 四域中 ≥2 域有困难，达中度或以上）+ Criterion B（病理性特质：antagonism 域 manipulativeness/deceitfulness/callousness/hostility，disinhibition 域 irresponsibility/impulsivity/risk taking；**≥6/7**）+ 注（≥18 岁）+ "Specify if: With psychopathic features"。

核验结果：**确认（VUMC 2014–15 镜像逐字复述 DSM-5 2013 Section III）**：
- "A. Moderate or greater impairment in personality functioning, manifested by characteristic difficulties in two or more of the following four areas: 1. Identity: Egocentrism; self-esteem derived from personal gain, power, or pleasure. 2. Self-direction: Goal setting based on personal gratification; absence of prosocial internal standards, associated with failure to conform to lawful or culturally normative ethical behavior. 3. Empathy: Lack of concern for feelings, needs, or suffering of others; lack of remorse after hurting or mistreating another. 4. Intimacy: Incapacity for mutually intimate relationships, as exploitation is a primary means of relating to others, including by deceit and coercion; use of dominance or intimidation to control others."
- "B. Six or more of the following seven pathological personality traits: 1. Manipulativeness (an aspect of Antagonism) ... 2. Callousness (an aspect of Antagonism) ... 3. Deceitfulness (an aspect of Antagonism) ... 4. Hostility (an aspect of Antagonism) ... 5. Risk taking (an aspect of Disinhibition) ... 6. Impulsivity (an aspect of Disinhibition) ... 7. Irresponsibility (an aspect of Disinhibition) ..."
- "Note. The individual is at least 18 years of age. Specify if: With psychopathic features."

来源：
- [VUMC 2014–15 Psychiatry Morning Report — AntisocialPD.pdf](https://www.vumc.org/psychiatry/sites/vumc.org.psychiatry/files/public_files/morningreport/Presentations2014-15/AntisocialPD.pdf)（Section II + Section III 双语准则逐字复述，二手但完整）
- 德语维基（德译二重佐证，且 "mindestens sechs der folgenden ... Merkmale" = ≥6）：[Dissoziale Persönlichkeitsstörung §DSM-5 Alternativ-Modell](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissoziale_Pers%C3%B6nlichkeitsst%C3%B6rung)

[需核验]：特质列举顺序与 DSM-5 Section III 官方排序是否一致（VUMC 顺序：Manipulativeness→Callousness→Deceitfulness→Hostility→Risk taking→Impulsivity→Irresponsibility）；≥6/7 阈值官方逐字（VUMC + 德维基一致，官方对页 [需核验]）。

## 五、品行障碍（CD）DSM-5 门槛概要（"15 岁前 CD 史"锚材料）

**命题 5**：CD = 反复而持续的、侵犯他人基本权利或重大适龄社会规范/规则的模式；近 12 个月内 4 组共 15 条中 ≥3 条（且 ≥1 条在近 6 个月）；加 B（功能损害）、C（≥18 岁者不得已满足 ASPD 标准）；按起病年龄分型 + "with limited prosocial emotions"（CU 特质）specifier（4 特征中 ≥2、持续 ≥12 个月、多关系/场景）。

核验结果：**确认（SAMHSA CBHSQ 2016 报告表 3.37 逐字引 DSM-5 2013，公共领域）**，逐字要点：
- A：“...as manifested by the presence of three (or more) of the following 15 criteria in the past 12 months from any of the categories below, with at least one criterion present in the past 6 months”
- 4 组（组名逐字）：Aggression to people and animals（条 1–7：bullies/threatens/intimidates; initiates physical fights; used a weapon; physically cruel to people; physically cruel to animals; stolen while confronting a victim; forced someone into sexual activity）；Destruction of property（条 8–9：fire setting with intent to cause serious damage; deliberately destroyed others’ property）；Deceitfulness or theft（条 10–12：broken into someone else’s house/building/car; often lies ... ("cons" others); stolen items of nontrivial value without confronting a victim）；Serious violations of rules（条 13–15：often stays out at night despite parental prohibitions, beginning before age 13; run away from home overnight ≥2 times; often truant from school, beginning before age 13）
- B：“The disturbance in behavior causes clinically significant impairment in social, academic, or occupational functioning.”
- C：“If the individual is age 18 years or older, criteria are not met for Antisocial Personality Disorder.”
- 起病分型：Childhood-Onset（312.81/F91.1，至少 1 条 <10 岁）、Adolescent-Onset（312.82/F91.2）、Unspecified Onset（312.89/F91.9）
- Specifier 逐字：“Specify if: With limited prosocial emotions: To qualify for this specifier, an individual must have displayed at least two of the following characteristics persistently over at least 12 months and in multiple relationships and settings.”；4 特征名：Lack of remorse or guilt / Callous—lack of empathy / Unconcerned about performance / Shallow or deficient affect（各自描述见原文，供"冷漠表征"侧对照）
- 严重度：Mild/Moderate/Severe 逐字（见原文）

来源：[SAMHSA CBHSQ — Impact of the DSM-IV to DSM-5 Changes on NSDUH, Table 3.37（DSM-IV vs DSM-5 CD 对照）](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK519704/table/ch3.t37/?report=objectonly)

[需核验]：CBHSQ 表引 DSM-5(2013)；DSM-5-TR(2022) CD 标准逐字是否全同（未取到 DSM-5-TR CD 免费镜像；无已公布标准变更线索，倾向未改）。

## 六、CD→ASPD 纵向流行病学

**命题 6**：约多少 % 品行障碍儿童最终发展成 ASPD；"多数 CD 儿童不发展为 ASPD"。

核验结果：**数值并存、口径差异大（供裁决，非单一值）**：
1. StatPearls（逐字，二手综述）："Nearly 25% of girls and 40% of boys diagnosed with conduct disorder eventually develop ASPD."
2. English Wikipedia（引二手）："About 25–40% of youths with conduct disorder will be diagnosed with ASPD in adulthood."（同见于 [LibreTexts 12.7 ASPD](https://socialsci.libretexts.org/Courses/Northeast_Wisconsin_Technical_College/Abnormal_Psychology_(NWTC)/12%3A_Personality_Disorders/12.07%3A_Antisocial_Personality_Disorder)）
3. Gelhorn et al. 2007（Compr Psychiatry；PMC 全文，逐字）："It has generally been accepted that about 40% of individuals with CD persist [to ASPD]."；其 NESARC 回顾样本得 75% 进展，并称 "the often quoted rate of persistence of CD to ASPD of 40%, such as in the AACAP conduct disorder practice parameters summary, may be a marked underestimate" —— 注意：高值来自成人回顾性报告（回忆偏倚 + CD 定义宽），与前瞻性研究不可直接比。
4. Robins (1966)（经 IACAPAP D.3 转述，逐字）："Robins (1966) first pointed out that **one half of conduct-problem children do not grow up to have antisocial personalities**."——即约半数不发展（该句隐含约半数发展，与 40–50% 量级一致）。

来源：
- [StatPearls ASPD（Prognosis）](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK546673/)
- [English Wikipedia — Antisocial personality disorder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisocial_personality_disorder)
- [Gelhorn et al. 2007, PMC2764329](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2764329/)
- [IACAPAP e-Textbook D.3 Conduct Disorder (2012)](https://dev.iacapap.org/_Resources/Persistent/6fb0edebbe8df29086098fe1f929e36e4bf5798e/D.3-CONDUCT-DISORDER-072012.pdf)

[需核验]："多数 CD 儿童不发展为 ASPD"的严格表述依赖所用口径：25–40%（多数不发展）vs NESARC 回顾 75%（少数不发展）——两数并存时不得单取其一当资格依据；建议以 StatPearls/Wikipedia 的 25–40% 为常引值并在文档中注明 NESARC 75% 为口径例外。
